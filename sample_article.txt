Amazon Bedrock is a fully managed service that makes foundation models (FMs) 
from leading AI companies accessible via an API. By choosing from a wide 
range of foundation models, developers can find the optimal model for their 
specific use case. This architecture leverages AWS Lambda to intercept 
S3 object creation events, extract the text content, and pass it directly 
to Bedrock for rapid summarization, keeping operational overhead close to zero.
